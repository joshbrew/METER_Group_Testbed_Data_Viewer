Guide

How it's laid out

  1. Header: the baseline logger, your loaded data, and three tabs.
  2. Triage, left: check one or more statuses in the health bar (DROPS and FAIL, say) to filter sensors. Any of keeps sensors with at least one checked status; Only these keeps sensors whose issues are exclusively the checked ones. The matrix and Findings both follow these filters.
  3. Triage, right: whatever you click on the left is charted against the baseline, with every measurement of a focused sensor underneath.
  4. Loggers holds the inventory plus battery and barometer charts. Sensor set charts every measurement of the filtered set.
